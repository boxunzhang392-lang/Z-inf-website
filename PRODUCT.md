# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the brief: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Motion for a greenfield build.

## Users

- Primary: high-school and university-age young people entering AI through research, project building, communities, hackathons, and demos.
- Secondary: schools seeking practical AI innovation programs and partners who can contribute mentorship, compute, technology, problems, industry access, or incubation opportunities.

## Product Purpose

Zinf discovers, trains, and organizes young AI builders, then helps them form teams and move from a first idea toward real projects, demonstrations, iteration, and early incubation. Homepage success means a new visitor understands this in five seconds and can choose a relevant path to participate.

## Positioning

Zinf connects the youth-facing entry points of schools, research training, communities, and events with young builders, teams, and projects. It is a youth AI project source and early incubation platform, not a traditional education institution or a generic AI software vendor.

## Operating Context

The ecosystem includes school collaboration, research training, youth communities, hackathons, young AI builders, teams, projects, demos, papers, and products. The intended project journey is: discover talent, train systematically, form teams, validate projects, iterate continuously, and incubate early.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage must support desktop, tablet, and mobile, with complex layouts becoming clear single-column flows below 768px.
- All interactions must support keyboards, reduced motion, readable Chinese and English, and WCAG AA contrast.
- Hero media must be preloaded and below-the-fold media lazy-loaded with dimensions reserved.
- Sound controls appear only when real audio exists. No audio exists yet, so no sound control is shown.
- Real projects, events, media, schools, partners, metrics, awards, mentors, contact details, and legal identifiers must not be fabricated.
- Missing factual content is represented through centralized, explicitly marked replacement data.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Zinf. Z means Generation Z; inf comes from Infinity.
- Core expression: “让 Z 世代创造 AI 的无限可能。”
- English expressions: “We discover and grow the next generation of AI builders.” and “From first idea to infinite possibility.”
- Binding direction from the supplied reference: dark monochrome, cinematic youth culture, experimental editorial typography, restrained AI Lab precision, giant Zinf wordmark, subtle fixed grain, and the blue sampled from the Zinf logo as the single auxiliary color.

## Product Principles

- Move young people from exposure to real project creation and sustained iteration.
- Keep learning connected to project creation, demonstration, iteration, and incubation.
- Treat youth as researchers, creators, and founders, not passive AI users.
- Make every unverified claim visibly replaceable instead of inventing proof.
- Balance cultural relevance with clear, accessible product communication.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA for body text and controls, preserve visible keyboard focus, provide semantic landmarks and descriptive media text, support reduced motion, and ensure the mobile experience is purpose-built rather than a scaled desktop layout. The audience includes minors, so the footer must reserve a clear minor-protection disclosure.
